# WeIntegral Website

The public website of WeIntegral, an IT services firm. It presents the firm's services to prospective clients and candidates and collects inbound inquiries.

## Language

**Visitor Request**:
An inquiry a site visitor submits through the contact ("Talk to an Expert") form: name, email, subject and message.
_Avoid_: Lead, contact submission, inquiry form

**Service**:
One of the firm's offerings: Application Development, Application Support, Quality Assurance & Testing, DevOps & Cloud, IT Staffing.
_Avoid_: Solution, product

**Industry**:
A market sector WeIntegral serves, shown to signal domain experience.
_Avoid_: Vertical, sector

**Client**:
An organization that has engaged WeIntegral for a Service.
_Avoid_: Customer, account

**Candidate Resources**:
Public reference links for job candidates, such as visa and H-1B lookups.
_Avoid_: Resources (unqualified)

**Internal Tools**:
Pages that link WeIntegral staff to company systems and recruiting lists; reachable by URL but not part of the public site.
_Avoid_: Management resources, mgmt resources
